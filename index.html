How matching works

Matched. A ticket references the exact wallet history event and the amount, unit and direction agree.

Match suggested. A ticket without a reference has a single wallet event of the same amount, unit and direction within five minutes. Only you can confirm it. A candidate shared by multiple tickets stays ambiguous.

Difference. Wallet net minus till net, calculated separately for each unit. A refund reduces the total. History is optional in NIP-60. These figures compare the supplied records and do not certify a Cashu balance.
